Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll online through our Register page, or visit any of our centres directly. Our counsellors will guide you through the process and help you choose the right program and batch timing.

What is the fee structure?

Fees vary by program and course type. We offer very competitive pricing along with scholarship programs and easy instalment options. Contact us for a detailed fee chart for your specific program.

Are online classes available?

Yes! We offer both live online classes and recorded sessions for all major programs. Online students also get access to study materials, mock tests, and doubt-clearing sessions.

What batch timings are available?

We offer morning (7–11 AM), afternoon, and evening (5–9 PM) batches Mon–Sat, plus weekend-only batches. Availability varies by program.

Do you provide study material?

All students receive comprehensive printed study material, practice question banks, and previous year papers. Online students additionally get digital PDFs and recorded video content.

Is there a free demo class?

Yes! We offer a free demo class for all programs. Register online or visit our centre and ask for a demo session — no commitment needed.
