About This Program

This program is designed for BCA and MCA students who need strong subject-wise coaching aligned to their university curriculum. Each subject is taught by a specialist faculty member with real-world experience, ensuring both theoretical understanding and practical application.

Classes are held in small batches of maximum 20 students to ensure every student receives individual attention. Doubt-clearing sessions are held weekly, and students get access to comprehensive study material, practice question banks, and lab facilities.

Who This Is For

This program is ideal for BCA (1st–3rd year) and MCA (1st–2nd year) students enrolled at any university in Hyderabad who want structured coaching, regular assessments, and expert guidance throughout their degree.
